## What Bespoke is

Bespoke helps you get a garment made the way you imagine it. You describe what you want in English or Hinglish, pin pictures on a moodboard, and Bespoke turns both into a clear specification that a tailor can cut from. It lists the garment, the neckline, the sleeves, the fit, the length, the fabric, the colour and the details you asked for, part by part. When the design is complete you get a tailor sheet that you can print or save as a PDF, and you can send the design to a maker. Bespoke is a design and specification tool: its job is to make sure the tailor understands you without a long back-and-forth.

## How Bespoke works in short

You type or speak a request such as "kurti chahiye, waist pe fitted, neeche se thoda loose, V neck thoda deep, no embroidery". Bespoke reads it and fills an order card, one row for each part of the garment. Where something important is missing, such as sleeve length, fabric or colour, it asks you a few short questions with options to choose from. You can tap any row on the order card to change it, and you can step back if you change your mind. Pictures on the moodboard add suggestions, but your own words always come first. At the end you have a specification and a tailor sheet.

## Who Bespoke is for

Bespoke has two kinds of users. Customers describe a garment, build a moodboard and get a tailor-ready specification. Makers, meaning designers and tailors, have their own account where they receive design requests from customers, read the specification, flag missing details and chat with the customer. You can use Bespoke to prepare a design even if you have not chosen a tailor yet, because the tailor sheet is something you can print and take anywhere.

## Languages Bespoke understands

You can write in English, in Hinglish (Hindi written in English letters) or in Hindi in Devanagari script, and you can mix them in one sentence. Words such as kurti, lehenga, dheela, gehra, baazu and kamar are understood without translation. Small spelling mistakes are usually forgiven, so "anarkalli" or "georget" still work. The tailor sheet can be shown wholly in English or wholly in Hindi, including the names of the parts, the values and the labels, so you can hand it to a tailor in the language they prefer.

## Where the assistant fits in

This help assistant answers questions about using Bespoke: how to describe a garment, how the moodboard works, how to take measurements, what the tailor sheet contains and how to work with a maker. It answers only from Bespoke's own help notes. If the notes do not contain the answer, it says so instead of guessing. It does not design the garment for you, so use the main conversation for that.
