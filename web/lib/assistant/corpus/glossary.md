## Glossary: Indian garments such as kurti, anarkali, lehenga and salwar suit

Bespoke knows these Indian garments, with the Hindi term printed on the Hindi tailor sheet. Kurti (कुर्ती) is a tunic top, and kurta is its longer or menswear form. Anarkali (अनारकली) is a long flared dress with a fitted top. Salwar suit (सलवार सूट) is a tunic with loose trousers and often a dupatta. Lehenga (लहंगा) is a long flared skirt, also called ghagra or chaniya choli. Saree blouse (ब्लाउज) is the fitted top worn with a saree, also called choli. Sherwani (शेरवानी) is a long formal coat. Nehru jacket (नेहरू जैकेट) is a short waistcoat or bandhgala.

## Glossary: bottoms and drapes

Churidar (चूड़ीदार) is a fitted trouser that gathers at the ankle. Sharara (शरारा) is a wide, flared trouser with fullness from the knee. Palazzo (प्लाज़ो) is a wide straight trouser. Dhoti pants are draped trousers. Saree (साड़ी) is a long draped cloth and dupatta (दुपट्टा) is a long scarf worn with a suit or lehenga. Kurta set (कुर्ता सेट) is a kurta with matching bottoms. Skirt is स्कर्ट, trousers are पैंट, jeans are जींस, and leggings, joggers, shorts and cargo pants are also known.

## Glossary: Western garments

Bespoke also knows everyday Western and winter wear: dress (ड्रेस), gown (गाउन), top (टॉप), shirt (शर्ट), t-shirt (टी-शर्ट), polo, tank top, crop top, corset, bodysuit, sweatshirt (स्वेटशर्ट), hoodie (हुडी), sweater (स्वेटर), cardigan (कार्डिगन), jacket (जैकेट), blazer (ब्लेज़र), coat (कोट), puffer jacket, shrug, cape, kaftan (कफ़्तान), jumpsuit (जंपसूट), co-ord set (को-ऑर्ड सेट), tracksuit, pyjama set and dungarees (डंगरी). In total there are 47 garment types.

## Glossary: cotton and everyday fabrics

Cotton (सूती) is breathable and good for summer and daily wear. Mulmul (मलमल), also called muslin or malmal, is a very soft, light cotton. Linen (लिनन) is cool and crisp and creases easily. Khadi (खादी) is hand-spun, hand-woven cotton. Rayon (रेयॉन), which includes viscose and modal, drapes softly. Denim (डेनिम) is a sturdy cotton twill. Polyester (पॉलिएस्टर) and lycra (लाइक्रा) are used for stretch and sportswear. Bespoke lists cotton, linen, mulmul, rayon and chiffon as summer fabrics, and polyester, rayon, crepe and denim for the monsoon.

## Glossary: silk, sheer and festive fabrics

Silk (रेशम), also called resham, is smooth and glossy. Banarasi (बनारसी), including brocade and jacquard, is a rich woven silk with patterns. Chanderi (चंदेरी) is a light sheer silk-cotton. Georgette (जॉर्जेट) and chiffon (शिफॉन) are light, flowing and slightly sheer. Organza (ऑर्गेंज़ा) is crisp and sheer. Crepe (क्रेप) has a fine crinkled texture. Satin (साटन) is glossy. Velvet (मखमल, also makhmal) is plush. Net (नेट) is an open mesh. These are common for weddings and festive wear.

## Glossary: winter fabrics

For cold days Bespoke lists fleece (फ़्लीस), wool (ऊन), terry (टेरी), corduroy (कॉरडरॉय), velvet and flannel (फ़लालैन). Cashmere (कश्मीरी) is a very soft fine wool, and tweed (ट्वीड) is a rough woven wool. Leather (चमड़ा) and faux fur (फ़र) are also in the list. Terry, cotton, rayon and flannel suit loungewear, and lycra, polyester and cotton suit gym wear. If you say "for winter" or "for the cold", Bespoke treats it as the occasion and suggests fabrics for it.

## Glossary: prints and embellishments

Prints include floral (फूलदार), striped (धारीदार), checked, polka dots, paisley (कैरी), bandhani (बांधनी, also bandhej or tie-dye), leheriya (लहरिया), block print, ikat (इकत), geometric and ombre. Embellishments include embroidery (कढ़ाई, also kadhai), zari (ज़री), gota patti (गोटा पत्ती), mirror work (शीशा काम), chikankari (चिकनकारी), sequins (सितारे), pearl work (मोती काम), zardozi (ज़रदोज़ी, also aari), thread work (धागा काम), lace (लेस), tassels (लटकन, latkan) and beadwork (मनके).

## Glossary: body and part words in Hinglish

These Hindi words come up in everyday talk about clothes, and the first group is understood in your messages to Bespoke. Gala means neckline, peeth or peeche means back, baazu or aasteen means sleeve, kamar means waist, kulha means hip, kandha means shoulder, chaati means chest or bust, daman or ghera means hem or flare, and chaak means slit. Naap means measurement, darzi means tailor, kapda means cloth or fabric, and rang means colour. Direction words are neeche (below), upar (above), aage (front) and peeche (behind).
