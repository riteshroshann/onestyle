## Privacy: where your words and pictures are read

Bespoke reads your words and your moodboard pictures in your own browser. A trained language model that tags garment words and a picture model both run on your device, so understanding what you wrote and what a picture shows does not depend on sending them anywhere. It works instantly and keeps working without a network connection once the page has loaded.

## The optional language model

Bespoke can optionally ask an outside language model to help with a request that its own on-device reading cannot name, such as an unusual phrase. When that is switched on, the text of your message and a short summary of the current design go to the model through a server function. If it is not configured, slow or failing, Bespoke carries on with its own on-device answer and you see no error. Whatever the model returns is checked against Bespoke's own list of garment words before it is used.

## What this help assistant does with your question

This help assistant works the same way: your question is checked for safety, personal details such as email addresses, mobile numbers and long digit strings are masked, and the question is matched against Bespoke's help notes. Only when the notes cover your question is a language model asked to phrase the answer. Please do not type personal details, passwords or payment information into the assistant, because it never needs them.

## Customer and maker accounts

Customers and makers use separate sign-ins. A maker only sees the requests that customers have sent to that maker. When you send a design, you share the specification and chat with that maker. If you do not want to share a design, do not send it; you can still use the tailor sheet on your own and print it for any tailor you choose.

## Where your designs and boards are kept

When you are logged in, your designs and moodboards are saved to your Bespoke account, so they open again on any device. Moodboard pictures are kept in private storage that only your account can read, and nobody else, makers included, can see your designs or boards unless you send a design to a maker.

When you are not logged in, your design lasts while the browser tab is open, and a new tab starts a fresh design. It is also kept on this device for two hours, so it is not lost if you create an account and confirm your email in a new tab; it is deleted after that, or as soon as you log in and it moves to your account. When you log out, the design in that tab is cleared, so the next person starts blank. Moodboards are kept in this browser on this device. The next time you log in on that device, those boards move to your account. Nothing you make while logged out is sent to Bespoke's servers.
